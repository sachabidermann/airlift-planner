## Legacy grid parser

Myanmar 2025 read through the legacy `grid.xml` parser (261x277) versus the modern CoverageJSON grid (261x277), 500 random points: mean difference +0.003, largest 0.26. The legacy parser is what reads the HayWired, ShakeOut, Cascadia and Loma Prieta grids.
